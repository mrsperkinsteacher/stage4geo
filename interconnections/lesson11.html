📋 Summary of Lesson

Today is a graphing skills lesson. You build a sector graph (also called a pie chart) from percentages: change each percentage into degrees using percentage x 3.6, measure each angle with a protractor, then label and colour each sector. The data shows how NBN connections reach Australian homes and businesses.

This matters because a graph only helps if it is accurate and honest. Checking that your degrees add up to 360, and that your percentages add up to 100, is how you know your graph can be trusted.

📖 Glossary

Sector graph - a circle divided into sectors, where each sector shows one part of a whole. Also called a pie chart.
Sector - one 'slice' of the circle, representing one category of the data.
Percentage - a share out of 100. All the percentages in a sector graph must add up to 100.
Protractor - an instrument for measuring and drawing angles in degrees.
Degrees - a full circle is 360 degrees, so every 1% of a sector graph is 3.6 degrees.

💡 Did You Know?

In June 2025, 99% of Australian adults had internet access at home (including through 4G or 5G mobile networks), and 97% used a mobile phone to go online. Adults used an average of 4.1 types of device to get online.

Source: Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA), How we use the internet, February 2026.

📰 In the News

The ACCC released its June quarter 2026 NBN report on 18 September 2026. It counted 8,817,314 NBN services. Fibre to the premises grew by about 145,000 services in the quarter, to 3,356,052, while fibre to the node and fibre to the curb both fell.

Source: Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC), NBN Wholesale Market Indicators Report, June quarter 2026 (18 September 2026).

🗺️ Where in the World?

All of Australia - about 5 in every 100 NBN services use fixed wireless or satellite. In June 2026 that was 388,636 on fixed wireless and 60,539 on satellite, out of 8.8 million services. The worksheet percentages are worked out from the ACCC counts and rounded to whole numbers.

Source: ACCC, NBN Wholesale Market Indicators Report, June quarter 2026.
