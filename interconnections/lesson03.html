📋 Summary of Lesson

Today you're investigating something surprising: your ordinary daily life - your phone, your breakfast, your clothes, the shows you watch - connects you to dozens of countries you've probably never thought about. You'll audit the apps and technology you use, then plot on a world map exactly where your food, clothes, media and family connections come from.

📖 Glossary

Audit - a careful check or count of something.
Continent - one of the world's seven large land masses.
Goods - physical things that are made, bought and sold.
Interdependence - when people and places rely on each other to meet their needs.

💡 Did You Know?

A single smartphone is typically made from around 30 different chemical elements/metals - roughly a quarter of the entire periodic table - including silicon, copper, gold, cobalt and rare earth elements, mostly mined in different countries.

Source: Ethical Consumer; Compound Interest, "The Chemical Elements of a Smartphone".

📰 In the News

11 April 2025: "Apple is ramping up production in India to avoid Donald Trump's tariffs on China" - Apple has been shifting a large share of iPhone assembly from China to India in response to US tariffs, a real, current example of how a supply-chain map can shift country to country almost overnight.

Source: ABC News, 11 April 2025.

2026: China tightened export controls on rare-earth elements such as gallium, germanium and dysprosium - the exact materials that make smartphone screens bright and colourful - creating fresh supply-chain risk for phone makers worldwide.

Source: S&P Global, "Rare Earth Supply Bottlenecks Set to Persist in 2026," and Clark Hill PLC industry briefing, 2026.

2026: The United States and partner countries signed new bilateral critical minerals agreements and launched the Forum on Resource Geostrategic Engagement (FORGE), aiming to build supply chains for tech materials that don't rely on any single country - a direct response to the risks your phone's supply chain faces.

Source: US Department of State, 2026 Critical Minerals Ministerial.

🗺️ Where in the World?

The Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC) - produces over half the world's cobalt and is a major source of tantalum (used in smartphone circuit boards), both essential minerals in the phone most students are holding.
