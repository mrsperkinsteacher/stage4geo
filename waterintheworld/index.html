Stage 4 Geography | Term 3

Water in the World

Where the world's water is, how it moves, why some places have too little, and how people care for it and manage it.

Water is everywhere, but not everyone has enough of it. This term you will find out where the world's water is, how the water cycle works, why water is valued so differently, what causes water scarcity and how people respond. You will also learn how Aboriginal Peoples care for water Country, get outside for fieldwork, and finish with a case study of Cyclone Debbie (2017).

Big questionWhy is water spread so unevenly around the world and within countries - and whose job is it to do something about it?
